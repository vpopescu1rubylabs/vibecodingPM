# NAT 3092 (06.2019) page 1 - rules as printed on the form

General: use a black or blue pen, BLOCK LETTERS, print X in the appropriate boxes. This declaration is NOT an application for a TFN. Read the instructions (page 2) incl. privacy statement before completing.

## Section A (payee)
- **A1 TFN**: 9 boxes (3-3-3). Alternatives (tick ONE instead of a TFN): (a) applied/enquired with the ATO for a new or existing TFN; (b) exemption: under 18 and not earning enough to pay tax; (c) exemption: receiving a pension, benefit or allowance. Modelled as 3 single-option checkbox fields; app should enforce "TFN or exactly one".
- **A2 Name**: Title (Mr/Mrs/Miss/Ms), surname, first given name, other given names (19 boxes each). Title and other names optional (our assumption).
- **A3 Home address in Australia**: 2 address rows, suburb row, state (3 boxes), postcode (4 boxes). Form says "home address", so avoid PO Boxes (assumption, not stated).
- **A4 Previous family name**: only if changed since last dealing with the ATO.
- **A5 Primary e-mail**: 2 rows x 19 boxes (38 chars max). Optional (assumption).
- **A6 DOB**: DD/MM/YYYY, 8 boxes.
- **A7 Basis paid (one only)**: full-time, part-time, labour hire, superannuation or annuity income stream, casual.
- **A8 Are you (one only)**: Australian resident for tax purposes / foreign resident for tax purposes / working holiday maker.
- **A9 Tax-free threshold**: Yes/No. Claim from one payer at a time unless total income from all sources is below the threshold. Answer No if foreign resident or working holiday maker, except a foreign resident receiving an Australian Government pension or allowance.
- **A10 HELP/VSL/FS/SSL/TSL debt**: Yes/No. If Yes the payer withholds extra for compulsory repayment.
- **Declaration by payee**: signature + date (DD/MM/YYYY). Penalties exist for false or misleading statements. "Once section A is completed and signed, give it to your payer to complete section B."

## Section B (payer; "if you are not lodging online")
- **B1 ABN or withholding payer number**: 11 boxes (2-3-3-3); branch number (3 boxes, if applicable).
- **B2** If no ABN/withholding payer number: have you applied for one? Yes/No.
- **B3** Legal name or registered business name (individual name if not in business): 3 rows x 19.
- **B4** Business address: 2 rows, suburb, state, postcode.
- **B5** Primary e-mail: 2 rows x 19.
- **B6** Contact person (19 boxes) and business phone number (10 boxes).
- **B7** "Print X in this box" if you no longer make payments to this payee.
- **Declaration by payer**: signature + date. Return the original to ATO, PO Box 9004, Penrith NSW 2740 (printed on form).

## Unsure / not on the form
- Which fields are mandatory is not stated on page 1 (only on page 2); required/optional above is inferred.
- Section B date/signature boxes are covered partly by an overlay artefact ("sign" tag and a dark stub) in the source image; rect is the full blue area.
- Max 10 phone boxes: AU landline with area code or mobile (both 10 digits). 8-digit numbers will not suggest an area code.
- Page 2 instructions (e.g. under-18 exemption detail) are not part of the image.
