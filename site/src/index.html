Everything You Need to Know About the TFN Declaration

What is a TFN declaration?

If you're starting a new job in Australia, you've likely been asked for a TFN declaration. It's the ATO form (NAT 3092) that gives your payer your tax file number (TFN) and the details they need to work out how much tax to withhold from your pay. Zendocs has the official layout (NAT 3092-06.2019) ready to fill in online. It is not an application for a TFN.

Why do I need to fill in a TFN declaration?

Three main reasons: First, it gives your payer your TFN – without it, they must withhold tax at the highest rate. Second, it tells them whether you're an Australian resident for tax purposes and whether to apply the tax-free threshold. And third, it flags any study or training loan (HELP, VSL, FS, SSL or TSL), so extra is withheld to cover your compulsory repayments. Almost every new employee is asked for one.

How do I fill in a TFN declaration?

It's pretty straightforward with Zendocs:

  1. Open the NAT 3092 form in our editor (each question opens one at a time)
  2. Enter your TFN, or tick one of the three exceptions if you don't have one yet
  3. Enter your name and your home address in Australia
  4. Add your date of birth and, if you like, your email address
  5. Choose how you're paid and your residency status
  6. Answer the tax-free threshold and study loan questions
  7. Sign and date the declaration, then download or print it

Who needs to fill in a TFN declaration?

Anyone starting to be paid by a new payer in Australia: full-time, part-time and casual employees, labour hire workers, and people starting a superannuation or annuity income stream. You complete Section A; your payer completes Section B.

When don't I need a TFN declaration?

Good news – you don't need a new one if you're staying with the same payer and nothing has changed. If you only want to change your tax-free threshold or study loan answers with a payer you already have, use a Withholding declaration (NAT 3093) instead. And if you're a contractor invoicing with your own ABN, you usually won't be asked for one.

When is a TFN declaration due?

There's no deadline for you as such, but here's the thing – until your payer has your TFN, they must withhold tax at the highest rate. So give it to them when you start, ideally before your first pay. If you've ticked that you've applied for a TFN, you have 28 days to give it to your payer.

Where do I get a blank NAT 3092 form?

Right here! Zendocs has the NAT 3092 form loaded and ready to fill in. No printing, and no handwriting in tiny boxes. The form is also published by the ATO at ato.gov.au.

How do I sign a TFN declaration online?

When you reach the declaration in the editor, type your name in a signature style or draw it with your finger or mouse. Your signature is added to the form on your device and is never uploaded. If your employer needs a handwritten signature, print the form and sign it by hand.

Where do I submit my completed TFN declaration?

Give it to your payer – the employer or organisation paying you. Don't send it to the ATO yourself; your payer completes Section B and takes it from there. Send it through a secure channel rather than plain email, then delete your downloaded copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the fillable TFN declaration available for free?

Yes. You can fill in, sign, download and print the NAT 3092 at no cost, with no account and nothing to install.

What happens if I hand in my TFN declaration late?

There's no fine for you, but until your payer has your TFN they must withhold tax at the highest rate. Any extra tax withheld is taken into account when you lodge your tax return.

How do I fill in a TFN declaration?

Open the form, answer the 19 questions one at a time, then sign and date Section A. Download or print it and give it to your payer.

Can I sign my TFN declaration online?

Yes. Type your name in a signature style or draw it with your finger or mouse. If your employer wants a handwritten signature, print the form and sign it by hand.

Who is required to fill in a TFN declaration?

Anyone starting with a new payer in Australia, including employees, labour hire workers and people starting a super or annuity income stream.

Does a TFN declaration expire? Do I need a new one every year?

No. It stays in place with that payer. You need a new one each time you start with a new payer. To change your tax-free threshold or study loan answers with your current payer, use a Withholding declaration (NAT 3093).

What happens if I don't provide my TFN?

You don't have to give your TFN, but if you don't (and you haven't ticked one of the exceptions), your payer must withhold tax at the highest rate. If you've applied for a TFN, you have 28 days to provide it.

Is it safe to type my TFN here?
