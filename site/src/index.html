What is the TFN declaration?

Employees complete it when starting a new job, so their employer knows how much tax to withhold. It is not an application for a tax file number.

Section A is for the payee. Section B is for the payer. Once section A is completed and signed, give it to your payer.

Independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Taxation Office. It does not lodge anything.

Is this an application for a TFN?

No. The declaration is not an application for a tax file number.

Who completes Section B?

Your payer, if they are not lodging online.

Is my progress saved?

Saved on this device for 24 hours so you can close the tab and come back. Your TFN, date of birth, email and signature are never stored, so you will need to re-enter them.

Is it safe to type my TFN here?

Your details stay on your device – nothing is uploaded. There is no account, no server storing your answers, and no analytics or third-party scripts on the form. Everything is drawn onto the form in your browser. Use “Start over” to wipe your saved draft, especially on a shared computer.

What should I do with the PDF?

Give it to your employer, who keeps it. Send it through a secure channel, not plain email. Then delete your downloaded copy. Do not send it to the ATO unless they ask you to.

Which version of the form is this?

NAT 3092-06.2019, page 1 only (Sections A and B). Check the latest version on the ATO website, ato.gov.au.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The form is built for phones as well as computers. You can type or draw your signature with your finger, then download or print the PDF.
